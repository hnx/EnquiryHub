Can we use any vertex angle to calculate the sine ratio in a triangle?

Can we choose any vertex angle of a triangle (x, y, z) and use the same pair of sides, AB as the opposite side and CD as the hypotenuse, to calculate its sine?

For example:

- sin(x) = AB/CD
- sin(y) = AB/CD
- sin(z) = AB/CD

Or does the opposite side change depending on which vertex angle we choose, even though the hypotenuse remains the same?
