When calculating the GCD of multiple terms, such as gcd(a₁, a₂, ..., a₈₈), we can express it by grouping the terms into separate pairs.

For example:

gcd(gcd(a₁, a₇₇), gcd(a₅₄, a₇₅), ...)

However, my doubt is whether we can reuse terms when forming these separate pairs.

For instance, after grouping (a₁, a₇₇), can we form additional pairs using either of those terms, such as (a₇₇, a₃₄) or (a₁, a₆₁)?

Are there any restrictions on reusing terms across different pairs when calculating the overall GCD?
